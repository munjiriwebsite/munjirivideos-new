Our Story

A still morning. Mist curls over the river. Hippos grunt. A monkey makes a move on your breakfast. We’re Katy and Eugene, and when we met in South Luangwa National Park in Zambia we used to spend time filming the amazing nature and everyday happenings for fun! Then we noticed something strange, the people and brands doing the most meaningful work, protecting nature, building communities, preserving craft, were often invisible. Why wasn’t anyone seeing them? We decided to change that. With our camera and editing skills, we set out to film their stories. But quickly, we realised it wasn’t just about beautiful visuals. To reach the right people, we had to learn more about storytelling, marketing, and connection. One of our first videos was for a handwoven natural rug brand. The right people saw it and it worked. Orders started coming in, so many that the founder joked another video might mean even more work than she could handle. That’s when we knew we were onto something. Since then, we’ve filmed stories all over, from Portugal to South Africa, UK to Kenya. We’ve shot in eco-lodges, filmed artisans at work, and helped camera-shy clients find their voice. And somehow… our work’s been shared with over 56.9 million people, including by Michelle Obama. Growth brought challenges. Pressure to become content machines, following the wrong advice, thinking about algorithms, made the flow disappear and with it, the spark that had started it all.The joy of creating, experimenting, telling stories drifted away. Then came a reminder. At a festa in our nearby town, most of the band looked tired, just going through the motions. But the guitarist he was so happy in the music, grinning like a kid, pure joy. And we realised we had to go back to that. Now, we help purpose-led brands stop overthinking, reconnect with their spark, and combine it with our storytelling, camera work, and a touch of marketing. So you can focus on your purpose, your expression, and your joy, while saving time and energy.
