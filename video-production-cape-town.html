Our Approach to Video Production Cape Town

Every project starts with a chat. We want to hear your story, what you stand for, your style, and what you want to achieve, so we can create a video that really feels like you.

We know video production in Cape Town can feel intimidating, from concept to final edit. That’s why we take care of all the technical details, allowing you to focus on what matters most: sharing your story.

We take care of everything in-house, including:

  • Coming up with the concept and planning
  • Finding the best spots around Cape Town
  • Directing and filming
  • Editing, colour grading, and sound
  • Creating versions for social media, including Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts

One day we’re shooting in a bright studio, the next we’re out in the Klein Karoo. We stay flexible, curious, and passionate about our craft, just like the people we work with.

Cape Town Video Production – Featured Work

Coral & Hive

We partnered with Coral & Hive for this video production Cape Town project to create a brand film that mirrors the rhythm and reverence of their work. Shot between their studio and expansive looms, the story follows each handmade rug, from ethically sourced fibre to the final woven piece.

Soul Design Jewellery

Shot in Kalk Bay and surrounding neighbourhoods, this video for Soul Design captured the beauty of everyday adornment. Handmade jewellery, African craftsmanship, and a team that gives back — this is the kind of video production Cape Town creatives come to us for.

With Dehye Collective, we created a vibrant visual showcase of their Ghanaian-inspired interiors and accessories. The content celebrated heritage, colour, and the tactile power of culture.
