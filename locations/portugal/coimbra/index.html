Videographer in Coimbra: Exploring

We decided to head to Coimbra to fix Eugene's broken shoe, and it was a good excuse for a stroll through some of Coimbra’s beautiful spots, such as...

The University of Coimbra

Officially founded in 1290, it’s the oldest university in Portugal and one of the oldest in Europe still in operation today.

Santa Cruz Church and Old Town

Santa Cruz was founded in 1131 and became one of the most important monasteries in the early days of the Portuguese kingdom. Both the first king of Portugal, Afonso Henriques, and his son Sancho I are buried there.

Mondego River

Locals and visitors use the riverbanks not just to walk, but to swim, paddleboard, have picnics, or watch migratory birds in the wetlands. Walking along the Mondego River, there are always opportunities for unique angles and natural moments to capture.

Botanical Gardens

Because it was originally designed for scientific study, the garden still includes plant collections from around the world and old greenhouses. Scenes like these can look incredible from above. You can see more of our drone videographer Portugal work.

Videographer Coimbra: Filming Tips

While waiting for the shoe repair, we took a walk and practiced a few quick filming techniques. Here are some simple tips that can make a big difference in your videos:

Close-Ups

Don’t be afraid to get close to your subject. Mix wide, medium, and close-up shots. Close-ups help your viewers notice details and make your videos feel more lively and interesting.

Play with Foreground for Depth

Try putting something in front of your lens, even a plant, railing, or fence. It adds depth to your shot and makes the scene feel more real and immersive.

Look for Leading Lines

Keep an eye out for lines around you, roads, railings, fences, or even paths. They naturally guide the viewer’s eye to the main subject and make your shots feel more intentional. You can explore more ideas like this in our video storytelling approach.
