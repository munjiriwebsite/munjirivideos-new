About Us

Because you should be out there doing the work you love, not stuck staring at your phone trying to film and edit it.

You’ve spent aaaages building your organisation or independent brand that you pour so much into. But now it seems like in order to grow, you also need to become a full-time content creator, it feels kind of fake and extremely tiring.

That's where we help! We turn what your audience actually cares about into beautiful, down-to-earth videos that make them slow down and really look at what you do.We handle the strategy and the camera stress, so you can focus on what you enjoy doing.

Over the last 8 years, our cameras have taken us all over the place from Portugal and South Africa to the UK and Kenya. We’ve been lucky enough to film for small independent brands, local charities, and even global names like CNBC and Michelle Obama.

Why Our Videos Work

Videos that feel true to you, but work to naturally grow your business We prefer a much simpler, low-profile approach. Instead of bringing an intimidating crew, a rigid script, and monster camera gear, we just blend into the background of your normal workday and capture things as they actually happen. Think of our style as documentary, but cinematic. Giving you videos that feel true to you, but work to naturally grow your business.

Low-pressure so you can actually just relax and enjoy it If you're sitting there thinking you’re way too camera-shy for this, we promise you almost every single person we work with tells us the exact same thing. We've worked with designers, social enterprise founders and safari guides who really don't like being in front of the camera. We take care of everything from the shot lists to guiding you through the interviews, keeping everything simple and low-pressure so you can actually just relax and enjoy it.

Our Story

Our story actually started in a hippo filled national park in Zambia. We were both just filming the amazing nature and everyday moments entirely for fun when we met.

But while we were there, we noticed something that didn’t feel right, the people doing the most meaningful work, things like protecting wildlife, building up communities, or preserving beautiful crafts, were completely invisible online. They were just way too busy doing the actual, hands-on work to stop and film it.We wanted to change that.

So, we combined our production skills with a real understanding of marketing and storytelling to help. Today, we help thoughtful businesses and organisations stop overthinking their content and just share their world naturally, so they can get back to doing what they love.
